MemCards is a free app to create your own cards and study them, designed for students.

Features:
- Create, edit and delete decks, and reuse a card in several decks
- Three deck types: vocabulary (one language per side), definitions and short answers
- Three study modes: flashcard (with swipe), written and multiple choice
- Options: swap term and definition, shuffle cards, spelling traps in multiple choice
- Resume your session where you left off
- Spaced repetition based on the SM-2 algorithm, with a daily limit per deck
- Share decks as JSON files (export and import)

Privacy:
MemCards works entirely offline. It does not need Internet access, collects no data, contains no ads and keeps your cards only on your phone.
